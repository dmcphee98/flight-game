# Ideas and Styling Guidelines

## Terminology
- #### Plane
  - A single unit of currency or passenger transference
- #### Customer/Passenger
  - Contained within planes and linearly totals to the value of a plane
- #### Unit/Resource
  - Alternative terminology for [customer/passenger](#customerpassenger)
- #### Airport
  - Denotes an endpoint for a plane, usually the place where points are tallied or money is accrued

## Game ideas
- Multiplayer
  - Money accruing monopoly-like simulator where the intent is to start from a small plane company and work your way up to a big tycoon.

  - Resource management game
